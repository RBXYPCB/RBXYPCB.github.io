Responsible Minerals Sourcing Policy

Version: 1.0 Effective Date: April 20, 2026

Applicable To: All employees, subsidiaries, affiliates, suppliers, and subcontractors of SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED

1. Policy Statement

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED is committed to conducting business in an ethical, socially responsible, and transparent manner. We recognize that the extraction, transport, and trade of certain minerals may contribute to serious human rights abuses, environmental degradation, and armed conflict in conflict-affected and high-risk areas (CAHRAs).

As a printed circuit board (PCB) manufacturer and responsible corporate citizen, we are committed to responsible mineral sourcing throughout our supply chain. We do not tolerate, and will not knowingly procure minerals that finance or benefit armed groups, or that are associated with serious human rights abuses, including forced labor, child labor, and other inhumane treatment.

This policy applies to all minerals covered by the Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI) reporting templates, including those under the Conflict Minerals Reporting Template (CMRT), Extended Minerals Reporting Template (EMRT), and Additional Minerals Reporting Template (AMRT).

2. Scope

This policy applies to all SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED facilities, subsidiaries, and affiliates worldwide, as well as to all direct and indirect suppliers, subcontractors, and business partners that provide materials, components, or services to SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED.

For the purposes of this policy, "covered minerals" include:

Template Minerals in Scope
CMRT (v6.6) Tin, Tungsten, Tantalum, Gold (3TG)
EMRT (v2.11) Cobalt, Mica, Lithium, Nickel, Copper, Graphite
AMRT (v1.31) All other minerals not covered by CMRT or EMRT

3. Guiding Standards and Frameworks

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED's responsible minerals sourcing program is guided by and aligned with the following internationally recognized standards and frameworks:

  • OECD Due Diligence Guidance for Responsible Supply Chains of Minerals from Conflict-Affected and High-Risk Areas – We adopt the five-step framework for mineral supply chain due diligence.
  • Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI) Reporting Templates – We use the latest versions of CMRT (v6.6), EMRT (v2.11), and AMRT (v1.31) as standardized tools for supply chain data collection.
  • Responsible Minerals Assurance Process (RMAP) – We support and encourage participation in RMAP, an independent, third-party audit program for smelters and refiners.
  • IPC-1755 Responsible Sourcing of Minerals Data Exchange Standard – Our reporting is aligned with this standard to ensure consistency and efficiency in data exchange.

4. Supply Chain Due Diligence

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED exercises due diligence in accordance with the OECD Guidance through the following measures:

4.1 Establish Strong Company Management Systems

  • We maintain and publicly communicate this Responsible Minerals Sourcing Policy.
  • We have established an internal management system responsible for implementing and monitoring this policy.
  • We incorporate responsible minerals sourcing requirements into supplier agreements and contracts.
  • We maintain a grievance mechanism to enable stakeholders to raise concerns regarding mineral sourcing practices.

4.2 Identify and Assess Risks in the Supply Chain

  • We require all direct suppliers to complete and submit the latest versions of CMRT (v6.6), EMRT (v2.11), and/or AMRT (v1.31), as applicable to their supplied materials.
  • We use the RMI Smelter Reference List to identify smelters, refiners, and processors in our supply chain.
  • We prioritize sourcing from smelters, refiners, and processors that are listed as RMAP "Conformant" or "Active."

4.3 Design and Implement a Strategy to Respond to Identified Risks

  • We develop and implement risk mitigation plans for identified supply chain risks.
  • We engage with suppliers to encourage the use of RMAP-conformant smelters and refiners.
  • We escalate identified risks to senior management and track progress toward mitigation.

4.4 Carry Out Independent Third-Party Audits of Smelters/Refiners/Processors

  • We support and encourage smelters, refiners, and processors in our supply chain to undergo independent third-party audits through the RMAP.
  • We use RMAP audit results as a key criterion for sourcing decisions.

4.5 Report Annually on Supply Chain Due Diligence

  • We publicly report on our responsible minerals sourcing efforts and due diligence activities.
  • Our CMRT (v6.6), EMRT (v2.11), and AMRT (v1.31) reports are available upon request or through our designated reporting channels.

5. Supplier Requirements

All suppliers of SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED are required to:

  1. Adopt a Responsible Minerals Sourcing Policy – Establish and implement their own policy consistent with the OECD Guidance and this policy.
  2. Complete RMI Reporting Templates – Submit the latest versions of CMRT (v6.6), EMRT (v2.11), and/or AMRT (v1.31) to SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED on a regular basis.
  3. Use RMAP-Conformant Smelters – Source minerals only from smelters, refiners, and processors that are conformant with or actively participating in the RMAP, or equivalent independent third-party audit programs.
  4. Provide Accurate and Timely Information – Respond to our due diligence inquiries promptly and ensure the accuracy and completeness of all reported information.
  5. Flow-Down Requirements – Cascade this policy and its requirements to their own suppliers and subcontractors.
  6. Cooperate in Risk Mitigation – Collaborate with SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED to identify, assess, and mitigate risks in the supply chain.

6. Mineral-Specific Commitments

6.1 Conflict Minerals (3TG – Tin, Tungsten, Tantalum, Gold) – CMRT v6.6

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED is committed to sourcing 3TG minerals only from smelters and refiners that have been validated as conflict-free through the RMAP or equivalent programs. We conduct Reasonable Country of Origin Inquiries (RCOI) to determine the origin of 3TG minerals in our supply chain. We do not knowingly source 3TG minerals from the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) or adjoining countries that directly or indirectly finance armed conflict.

6.2 Extended Minerals (Cobalt, Mica, Lithium, Nickel, Copper, Graphite) – EMRT v2.11

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED is committed to responsible sourcing of extended minerals, recognizing the increased regulatory and stakeholder focus on these materials, particularly in battery and electronics supply chains. We require suppliers to report on cobalt, mica, lithium, nickel, copper, and graphite using EMRT v2.11 and to source these minerals from smelters and processors that have undergone independent third-party due diligence validation.

6.3 Additional Minerals – AMRT v1.31

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED is committed to responsible sourcing of all other minerals not covered by CMRT or EMRT. We use AMRT v1.31 to collect supply chain information for additional minerals and to support the identification of smelters, refiners, and processors for potential RMAP audit.

7. Template Compliance and Version Control

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED requires the use of the latest RMI reporting template versions for all supply chain reporting:

Template Version Release Date Key Features
CMRT v6.6 April 17, 2026 Expanded Product List fields; ISO short-name standardization; updated Smelter Reference List
EMRT v2.11 April 17, 2026 ISO short-name updates; corrected date input; updated Smelter Reference List
AMRT v1.31 April 17, 2026 ISO short-name updates; corrected date input; updated Smelter Reference List

Suppliers are expected to phase out older template versions and transition to the latest versions for all new submissions. SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED will update its reporting processes to reflect the latest template versions and will communicate any changes to suppliers accordingly.

8. Governance and Accountability

  • Responsibility: The Department Supply Chain Management is responsible for the implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of this policy.
  • Training: SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED provides training to relevant employees and suppliers on responsible minerals sourcing requirements and RMI reporting templates.
  • Monitoring: We monitor supplier compliance through regular reviews of CMRT, EMRT, and AMRT submissions, and conduct follow-up communications with suppliers reporting non-conformant smelters.
  • Non-Compliance: Failure to comply with this policy may result in corrective action, up to and including termination of the supplier relationship.

9. Reporting and Transparency

SHENZHEN RUIBOXINGYUAN ELECTRONICS CO., LIMITED is committed to transparency in our responsible minerals sourcing efforts. We:

  • Publish this Responsible Minerals Sourcing Policy on our corporate website.
  • Make our CMRT (v6.6), EMRT (v2.11), and AMRT (v1.31) reports available to customers and stakeholders upon request.
  • Report annually on our due diligence activities and supply chain risk identification and mitigation efforts.

10. Review and Continuous Improvement

This policy will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in:

  • Regulatory requirements and legal obligations
  • RMI reporting template versions and standards
  • Industry best practices and emerging risks
  • Stakeholder expectations and customer requirements

This policy is effective as of April 20, 2026 and supersedes all previous versions.
Based on OECD Due Diligence Guidance and aligned with RMI CMRT v6.6, EMRT v2.11, and AMRT v1.31.
